TOURNAMENT RULES.

Please read the rules before entering a match. Your participation and payment are subject to the rules below.

01

No rules

No additional gameplay rules are imposed by BOOYAH ARENA.

02

Record your gameplay

Record your gameplay because hackers may enter the match. If you submit clear proof and the proof is verified by the admin, the admin can remove the offending player.

03

Participate on time

Participate in your match at the scheduled time. If you do not participate on time, your entry amount will not be returned.

04

Admin may adjust prizes

If a BR match does not reach the expected player count, for example 28/48 players, the admin may change the entry, prize, or per-kill amount for that match. Any change will be managed by the admin before the match.
